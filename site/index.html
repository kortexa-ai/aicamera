◉

Your camera deserves some privacy.

Local models process on your Mac. AI Camera does not record raw camera or microphone media. Optional OpenAI features send microphone audio to OpenAI when enabled; the supported conversation setup does not upload camera frames.

Read about privacy ↗

A small alpha, out in the world.

There will be rough edges. Intel runtime coverage and compatibility across call apps are still being tested. Use AI Camera’s own Mute control to hide captions—muting only in your call app does not yet do that. System-component changes may need macOS approval or a restart.
