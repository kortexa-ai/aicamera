A little intelligence in your camera.

AI Camera adds live captions, translation, gestures, and a voice agent to your camera feed.

This installer places AI Camera in Applications, or upgrades an existing copy. Your settings, downloaded models, and login are kept.

Camera and microphone setup happens separately inside the app. This installer does not install, update, or remove those system components.

0.2.0 is an early alpha. Expect rough edges. Installation and cleanup instructions are included in the release notes.
